01 • The Problem & The Artemis Lunar Dilemma

High Oxygen on the Moon vs. Fragmented ISS Data

To reduce astronaut spacesuit pre-breathing time, future Lunar Bases & Pressurized Rovers will operate at ≥30% O₂ and ≤10.2 psi (>40% more oxygen than Earth), dramatically increasing material flammability.

1. Incompatible Data Silos in PSI

Terabytes of files across BASS-II, SAFFIRE-II, SAME-R, FLEX-2, and ACME use disparate units (bar, atm, psi, mole fraction XO2 vs. % O₂, fan steps vs. cm/s), making cross-study comparison slow and error-prone.

2. Zero Lunar Partial-Gravity (1/6g) Data

PSI currently contains 0g orbital data and 1g baselines, but no direct 1/6g lunar combustion data yet. Engineers need a systematic tool to bound intermediate-gravity fire risk today.
